# 🌟 1. Our Vision: One Ummah, United Through Knowledge

> "And hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided."
>
> — Quran 3:103

The Muslim Ummah shares one Quran, one Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, and a common foundation of faith. Yet, throughout history, differences in interpretation, jurisprudence and scholarly methodology have shaped various schools of thought and traditions.

Our vision is to create a global platform where Muslims can explore authentic Islamic knowledge without unnecessary sectarian hostility, misinformation or artificial barriers.

**Our mission is not to erase legitimate scholarly differences. It is to make knowledge accessible, encourage respectful dialogue, and help Muslims understand the evidence and reasoning behind different scholarly positions.**

We believe that:

- The Quran and authentic Sunnah are fundamental sources of Islamic guidance.
- Scholarly differences should be presented honestly and respectfully.
- Every religious claim should have traceable references.
- Islamic knowledge should be accessible regardless of language, location or background.
- Research should be transparent, verifiable and open to correction.
- Technology should serve Islamic knowledge, not replace qualified scholarship.

This is more than another Islamic application.

**It is an attempt to build a global, interconnected Islamic knowledge ecosystem.**

A platform where anyone can read, research, ask questions, contribute, learn and participate in preserving Islamic knowledge.

# 📚 4. The Hadith Library & Verification System

A comprehensive, structured Hadith research database.

### Initial Collections

The initial focus includes the six major Sunni Hadith collections:

1. Sahih al-Bukhari
2. Sahih Muslim
3. Sunan Abu Dawud
4. Jami' at-Tirmidhi
5. Sunan an-Nasa'i
6. Sunan Ibn Majah

Additional collections will include:

- Musnad Ahmad.
- Muwatta Imam Malik.
- Mishkat al-Masabih.
- Other recognized Hadith collections.

### Hadith Database Features

Every Hadith record should support:

- Original Arabic text.
- Multiple translations.
- Book and chapter information.
- Hadith numbering.
- Narrator information.
- Isnad (chain of narration).
- Matn (Hadith text).
- Source collection and edition.
- Scholarly grading.
- Related Hadith references.
- Cross-references and variations.

### Advanced Hadith Verification

Hadith grading is a scholarly discipline. Different scholars may evaluate the same narration differently.

Therefore, the platform will not force every Hadith into one universal grading field.

Instead, the system will support multiple grading records.

Each grading record may contain:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Hadith ID | Internal reference |
| Grading | Sahih, Hasan, Da'if, etc. |
| Scholar | Name of the grading scholar |
| Source | Original reference |
| Explanation | Reasoning, where available |
| Date/Edition | Publication information |
| Verification | Review status |

### Narrator Research

Users and researchers will be able to explore:

- Narrator biographies.
- Narrator reliability evaluations.
- Historical information.
- Teacher-student relationships.
- Narrator connections within Isnad chains.
- Scholarly disagreements regarding narrators.

**Important:** Hadith text, translation, chain analysis and grading are distinct pieces of information. Their sources and scholarly attributions must remain identifiable.

# ⚖️ 5. Ahkam-o-Masail: Comparative Islamic Jurisprudence

One of the central features of this project is **Fiqh-e-Muqaran (Comparative Jurisprudence).**

Instead of presenting only one jurisprudential position, the platform aims to document different scholarly opinions with their supporting evidence and reasoning.

### Supported Research Traditions

The initial comparative framework will focus on:

- Hanafi.
- Maliki.
- Shafi'i.
- Hanbali.

Other scholarly traditions and individual scholarly positions may also be included as reliable, properly sourced research becomes available.

### How Comparative Fiqh Will Work

Consider a question:

**Does touching a woman invalidate Wudu?**

The platform will present a structured comparison:

| Research Area | Information |
|---|---|
| Hanafi | Scholarly position and supporting evidence |
| Maliki | Scholarly position and supporting evidence |
| Shafi'i | Scholarly position and supporting evidence |
| Hanbali | Scholarly position and supporting evidence |
| Quran | Relevant Ayat and interpretations |
| Hadith | Relevant narrations and gradings |
| Scholarly Analysis | Reasoning and methodological differences |

*This is an illustrative interface example, not a published Fatwa or a complete representation of these schools' positions.*

### Every Fiqh Article Should Include

1. The actual question or Mas'ala.
2. A clear description of the issue.
3. Relevant Quranic references.
4. Relevant Hadith references.
5. Hadith grading and attribution.
6. Opinions of recognized scholars.
7. Evidence used by each position.
8. Differences in interpretation.
9. Relevant Usul al-Fiqh principles.
10. References to original scholarly works.
11. Researcher and reviewer information.
12. Verification and revision history.

### Our Principle

We will not artificially force a consensus where scholarly disagreement exists.

The objective is to help users understand:

- What scholars said.
- Why they said it.
- Which evidence they considered.
- Where their interpretations differ.
- Which conclusions are established and which remain disputed.

The platform will distinguish educational research from personal Fatwa services.

# 🔬 6. Tehqiq & Islamic Research System

A dedicated research environment where users can publish Islamic research, submit corrections and contribute scholarly findings.

### Research Publication

Registered users will be able to publish:

- Hadith research.
- Quranic research.
- Narrator verification.
- Fiqh investigations.
- Source corrections.
- Translation corrections.
- References to original Islamic books.
- Scholarly comparisons.
- Detailed research articles.

### Research Article Structure

Every Tehqiq submission should support:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Title | Research title |
| Author | Contributor information |
| Category | Quran, Hadith, Fiqh, etc. |
| Research Question | What is being investigated? |
| Findings | Research conclusions |
| Evidence | Supporting references |
| Sources | Books, editions and original citations |
| Methodology | Research approach |
| Attachments | Supporting documents |
| Review Status | Pending, approved, rejected or revised |

### Correction Submission System

Users can report:

- Incorrect Arabic text.
- Translation mistakes.
- Incorrect Hadith references.
- Missing narrators.
- Incorrect chapter information.
- Incomplete source information.
- Incorrect scholarly attributions.
- Database inconsistencies.

Every correction will enter a review workflow.

Original content will not silently disappear. The system will preserve previous versions and record who submitted, reviewed and approved each change.

- Published research.

The platform may provide:

- Weekly contributor rankings.
- Monthly contributor rankings.
- Most helpful researchers.
- Most approved corrections.
- Top translators.
- Top community reviewers.

### Anti-Abuse & Academic Integrity

Points must reward useful work, not religious popularity.

Therefore:

- Self-approved contributions are prohibited.
- Duplicate submissions do not earn repeated rewards.

- Rejected or reverted contributions may have their points adjusted.
- Review decisions must be recorded.
- Users cannot purchase scholarly credibility.
- Points and badges do not represent Islamic authority.
- A high-ranking contributor cannot automatically approve Fatwas or religious rulings.

**A contribution's scholarly value must be determined by evidence and qualified review, never by its popularity.**

# 🤖 10. AI-Assisted Islamic Research Assistant

Artificial Intelligence will be integrated as a research assistant to make Islamic knowledge easier to discover and understand.

### Planned Capabilities

Users will be able to ask questions in natural language, including Urdu, Arabic and English.

Example:

"What are the different scholarly opinions about raising hands during Salah?"

The assistant should:

1. Understand the user's question.
2. Search the verified Islamic database.
3. Retrieve relevant Quranic references.
4. Retrieve relevant Hadith.
5. Identify scholarly opinions.
6. Present comparative explanations.
7. Provide direct source references.
8. Clearly identify unresolved scholarly disagreements.

### AI Research Requirements

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- Source-grounded responses.
- Direct links to database references.
- Clear distinction between evidence and interpretation.
- Multilingual question support.
- Source verification.
- Transparent uncertainty handling.
- No fabricated Hadith or Quranic references.

### Important Religious Safeguard

AI must not independently manufacture Islamic rulings or present its generated interpretations as authoritative Fatwas.

If sufficient evidence is unavailable, the assistant must clearly state that limitation.

For sensitive or disputed religious matters, it should direct users towards qualified scholars.

**AI is a tool for discovering and organizing knowledge, not a replacement for Islamic scholarship.**

# 📚 14. Source Provenance, Copyright & Licensing

Authenticity and traceability are fundamental requirements.

Every dataset and research contribution should have identifiable origins.

### Source Information

Where applicable, records should contain:

- Original book or collection.
- Author or compiler.
- Publisher.
- Edition.
- Volume and page.
- Hadith or Ayah reference.
- Translator.
- Original publication language.
- Digital source.
- Copyright and license information.

### Content Licensing

Not all Islamic texts and translations have identical licensing conditions.

The project will:

- Prefer public-domain and openly licensed resources.
- Record licenses for third-party datasets.
- Preserve required attribution.
- Respect translator and publisher rights.
- Avoid redistributing copyrighted material without permission.
- Document restrictions on individual datasets.

### Data Attribution

Contributors must not remove original source information or present third-party translations as their own work.

Each dataset should have a corresponding source and licensing record.

# 🛡️ 15. Scholarly Review & Editorial Governance

An open-source platform needs a transparent system for reviewing religious research.

Open contributions do not mean that every submission is automatically correct.

### Review Workflow

```text
Contributor Submission
        |
        ▼
Automated Validation
        |
        ▼
Initial Moderation
        |
        ▼
Research / Scholarly Review
        |
        ├── Approved
        |
        ├── Changes Requested
        |
        └── Rejected
        |
        ▼
Publication & Version History
```

### Reviewer Responsibilities

Authorized reviewers may:

- Verify source references.
- Examine translations.
- Review Hadith attributions.
- Evaluate research methodology.
- Identify missing evidence.
- Request corrections.
- Approve or reject submissions.

### Transparency

Published research should display:

- Original author.
- Reviewers, where disclosure is permitted.
- Publication date.
- Last modification date.
- Review status.
- Supporting references.
- Revision history.

A rejected submission may remain available to its author with feedback, but rejected material must not be presented as verified research.

The platform should also provide an appeals process for disputed moderation and editorial decisions.

# ❓ 21. Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this project affiliated with a particular Islamic sect?

The project's objective is to provide a respectful, evidence-based environment that documents different scholarly traditions and positions without unnecessary sectarian bias.

### Will the platform issue Fatwas?

The platform is primarily an educational and research resource. Community discussions and AI-generated responses are not authoritative Fatwas. Any dedicated Fatwa service would require qualified scholars and its own governance.

### Can anyone register?

Yes. The planned web and mobile platforms will allow public registration, reading, asking questions and participating in community activities.

### Can ordinary users publish research?

Yes. Registered contributors will be able to submit research and corrections. Submissions will undergo the appropriate review process before receiving verified status.

### Can contributors earn points?

Yes. The contribution system will recognize useful research, approved corrections and other meaningful contributions. Points do not establish religious authority.

### Will the database and API be free?

The project aims to provide free access to public Islamic knowledge and developer APIs, subject to infrastructure and licensing limitations.

### Will the application work offline?

Yes. Offline Quran, Hadith and selected research content are important development objectives.

### Can I contribute without being a developer?

Absolutely. Translation, data verification, research, documentation and scholarly review are equally important.
